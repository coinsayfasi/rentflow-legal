Delete Your Account

RentFlow: Property Manager (TabServe) — Last updated: June 7, 2026

This page explains how you can request deletion of your RentFlow: Property Manager account and the associated data. RentFlow is developed by TabServe.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (recommended)

You can permanently delete your account directly from within the RentFlow app:

  1. Open the RentFlow app and sign in.
  2. Go to the More tab, then open Settings.
  3. Scroll to the Account section and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the action. Your account and all associated data are permanently deleted.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you are unable to access the app, send a deletion request to support@tabserve.com.tr from the email address associated with your account, with the subject line "Delete my RentFlow account". We will verify your identity and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What data is deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove all data linked to your account, including:

  • Your account and profile information (email, name)
  • Properties, units, tenants, and lease records
  • Rent payments and financial records
  • Maintenance requests and uploaded photos
  • Documents and files stored in the app
  • Messages and any other content you created

Deletion is immediate and irreversible. Once your account is deleted, this data cannot be recovered.

Data retention

Account and personal data are deleted immediately upon confirmation, and any residual copies in encrypted backups are purged within 30 days. We may retain a limited set of records where required by law (for example, transaction or tax-related records), for the minimum period required, after which they are also deleted.

Contact

For any questions about account or data deletion, contact us at support@tabserve.com.tr.
