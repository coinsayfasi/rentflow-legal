Frequently asked questions

Is RentFlow free?
Yes — RentFlow is free to start on the App Store and Google Play. A Pro plan unlocks unlimited properties and advanced automation.
Who is RentFlow for?
RentFlow is for independent landlords and small real-estate investors who want to track rent, tenants, leases and expenses in one place — with a free tenant portal and built-in calculators.
Does RentFlow have a tenant portal?
Yes. Each tenant gets their own portal to view payments, upload receipts, message you and access documents — no extra app needed.
What can RentFlow calculate?
Rental yield, cash flow, ROI and legal rent increases across 18+ countries — all free, built into the app.
What platforms is RentFlow on?
RentFlow is available on both iOS (App Store) and Android (Google Play).
How is my property and tenant data stored?
RentFlow stores property, tenant and lease data securely in the cloud (Supabase/PostgreSQL with row-level security), so it syncs across your devices and is backed up automatically.
Does RentFlow work offline?
You can view previously loaded properties, tenants and payment history offline; adding new records or syncing changes requires an internet connection.
